01 / The essentials

The little marks
that do a lot.

Markdown uses punctuation you already know. On the left is what you type; on the right is what readers see.

01

Headings

Start a line with #. More hashes make a smaller heading. Keep one main # heading per page.

You write
## A section
It becomes

A section

02

Emphasis

Wrap a word in one asterisk for italics, or two for bold. Put the marks right next to the words.

You write
*gentle* **reminder**
It becomes
gentle reminder
03

Lists

Use - for a bullet list, or a number and a period for steps. Leave a space after the marker.

You write
- notice
- collect
- share
It becomes
  • notice
  • collect
  • share
04

Links

Put the readable label in square brackets, then the web address in parentheses. Images work similarly, with ! in front.

You write
[A helpful guide](https://example.com)
It becomes
A helpful guide
05

Quotes & code

Begin a quote with >. Use backticks for a short code word, or three backticks on their own lines for a code block.

You write
> Keep it simple.

`print("hi")`
It becomes
Keep it simple.
print("hi")
06

Line breaks & dividers

Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Three hyphens on a line make a horizontal divider.

You write
A first thought.

---

A fresh start.
It becomes
A first thought.
A fresh start.
